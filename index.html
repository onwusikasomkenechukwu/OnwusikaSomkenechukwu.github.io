Intro

Curious by nature and driven by purpose, I’m a Mechanical Engineering major at Howard University with a growing passion for computing and intelligent systems. I’m especially interested in the intersection of robotics, machine learning, and real-world applications — from autonomous systems to health-focused technologies. By the way, check out my awesome work.

I've explored data science, AI model-building, and hands-on engineering through internships and personal projects. Whether it's developing a heart arrhythmia detection model or prototyping in hardware labs, I enjoy solving complex problems that sit at the edge of disciplines.

Currently seeking internships and research opportunities where I can contribute meaningfully, learn rapidly, and grow in my mission to build solutions that are not just functional, but thoughtful.

Antoine de Saint-Exupéry once wrote, "Perfection is achieved, not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away." I bring this mindset to every project — striving for clarity, simplicity, and purpose in all I build.
